# DTF print-preparation standards

## Required checks

- Use the exact approved catalog design ID.
- Preserve the source artwork; work only from a duplicate.
- Confirm final physical dimensions in millimeters before export.
- Keep transparent background where the design calls for it; do not add a white rectangle.
- Inspect edges at 100% and 200% for halos, stray pixels, and unintended transparency.
- Confirm artwork orientation and placement direction before printing.
- Use the printer/operator’s approved color profile and white-ink settings; do not guess device-specific settings.
- Keep text and important details inside the safe area appropriate to the garment and placement.
- Embed or outline fonts in editable working files, and rasterize only in the approved export path.
- Record export dimensions, resolution, color mode, transparency, and revision.

## Export naming

`<design-id>_<placement>_<width-mm>_<revision>_print.<ext>`

Example: `MTW-001_front_280mm_rev01_print.png`

## Release gate

An export is not production-ready until the source is verified, proof is approved, preflight is complete, and the final file is traceable to a catalog ID. If the original asset is unavailable, stop at intake and mark the order blocked rather than recreating the artwork.
